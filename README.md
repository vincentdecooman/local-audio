# Local Audio

A free menu bar app for recording Microsoft Teams desktop calls on a Mac. Local Audio can capture your microphone and Teams audio in one M4A saved on your Mac.

[Download Local Audio](https://github.com/vincentdecooman/local-audio/releases/latest)

Requires macOS 15 or later. Open the downloaded DMG and drag Local Audio into Applications. Give the app microphone and system audio permission when macOS asks.
